About Gitata

Gitata sits within Karu Local Government Area, Nasarawa State — part of the fast-growing Karu–Mararaba–Nyanya corridor bordering Abuja. This page is Discover Gitata's front door: who we are, and what this site sets out to do.

The place

A growing corner of Nasarawa State.

Gitata's roots trace back to the Gwandara people, who left Kano around 1470–1500 AD rather than abandon their traditions, and to the Yeskwa (Nyankpa) people, who settled Gitata's hills in the late 1600s and built a life alongside them through trade and intermarriage. The name itself comes from Gidzi-atata — "house in a plain land" — reshaped by a colonial road-builder's ear into the name it carries today.

Discover Gitata exists to put that identity in writing — properly researched, regularly updated, and easy for anyone to find. Read the full account on the History & Heritage page.

Why this exists

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To research, document, and preserve the history, traditional leadership, culture, and development of Gitata, and to make that record freely and reliably accessible — to residents, to the diaspora, and to anyone encountering Gitata for the first time.

Our Vision

A Gitata where every generation can trace where it comes from, where traditional leadership and community achievement are properly recorded, and where the district's culture and opportunities are visible to the wider world.
