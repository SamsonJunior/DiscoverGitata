Business & Investment

An agrarian economy turning into a trading hub — ginger, grain, and a growing retail corridor, all within reach of Abuja.

Where the economy sits

Sectors to know.

Agriculture

Gitata is a major regional producer of ginger, yams, maize, cassava, guinea corn, and beans — the foundation of the local economy.

Trade & markets

Sabon Kasuwa Gitata and the new Gitata International Beans Market pull buyers in from Keffi, central Nasarawa, and Abuja.

Retail & services

A growing town-centre corridor of provision stores, pharmacies, tailoring, phone repair, and POS banking agents.

Transport & logistics

Commercial trucking and Okada/Keke networks moving crops and people between Gitata's satellite villages and the wider region.

The agricultural engine

Gitata's breadbasket role.

The ginger belt

Gitata sits within a highly profitable ginger-producing belt shared with neighbouring districts. Local farmers cultivate premium, aromatic ginger sought after by national and international buyers for export, alongside yams, maize, cassava, and guinea corn.

Farmer development programmes

Agricultural development initiatives — including the FSSS AVISA programme active in Zango-Gitata — train local farmers in modern agronomy, pest control, and business planning, strengthening the district's output over time.

Trading & markets

Where the deals happen.

Sabon Kasuwa Gitata

The district's central marketplace — "New Market Gitata" — comes alive on designated market days. Wholesale buyers and middlemen gather here to aggregate yams, grains, and ginger for onward transport to Keffi, central Nasarawa, and Abuja.

Gitata International Beans Market

A newer development in the district's economy — a dedicated trading hub for beans, millet, and rice, aimed at reducing post-harvest losses and opening the area up to regional investors.

Everyday commerce

Micro-enterprises & services.

Since Gitata's connection to the national electricity grid, the town centre has seen a steady rise in retail and technical services.

Agro-processing

Cassava mills for garri and flour, plus ginger-drying operations preparing crops for export.

Retail & grocery

Provision stores, pharmacies, and daily kiosks supplying households across the district.

Fashion & tailoring

Native tailors and fabric retailers serving the local and regional Okrika trade.

Technical & artisan trades

Welding and fabrication workshops, barbing and hairdressing salons, and phone repair outlets.

POS banking agents have also become common across the town centre, handling cash withdrawals, transfers, and bill payments where formal bank branches are scarce.

For investors

Why look at Gitata.

Gitata sits close enough to Abuja to matter, with an economy already anchored in ginger export, grain aggregation, and the newly developing International Beans Market. Opportunities worth a closer look include crop storage and warehousing, agro-processing capacity, and logistics serving the district's satellite villages.
